WHO WE ARE

Northern Women Assembly (NWA)

A women-focused organization working towards empowerment, education, unity, peace and community development.

Welcome to NWA

Northern Women Assembly is an organization established to bring women together, strengthen their voices and create opportunities for personal growth, practical skills, education and meaningful participation in society.

We believe every woman has the ability to contribute positively to her family, community and nation. Our goal is to encourage women to discover their abilities, overcome challenges and become confident, independent and responsible members of society.

Through our coordination network, we seek to connect women, encourage collaboration and promote positive change across the 19 states represented by our organization's coordination structure.

OUR BEGINNING

Our History

Northern Women Assembly was established on 17 August 2018, with its headquarters at No. 23, Hotoro GRA, Kano State, Nigeria.

The organization was founded to bring women together, promote peace and unity, and create a platform where women could recognize their worth and develop their abilities.

NWA recognizes that education extends beyond formal schooling. Vocational training, useful information, literacy, mentorship and lifelong learning can all help women improve their lives.

We believe that supporting women's development can strengthen families, encourage community participation and contribute to national progress.

OUR ORGANIZATIONAL REACH

Connecting Women Across 19 States

Northern Women Assembly operates from its headquarters in Hotoro GRA, Kano State, through a network of 19 state coordinators representing the states covered by its coordination structure.

Our coordinators help connect the organization with women and communities, share information, identify community needs and encourage collaboration.

KNOWLEDGE CHANGES LIVES

Our Commitment to Education

We believe education is a powerful tool for changing lives. A woman's opportunities should not be limited by her level of formal education, financial circumstances or background.

We encourage formal education where possible, alongside vocational training, literacy programmes, mentorship and lifelong learning.

Education does not end in the classroom, and a woman's potential does not end in the home.
