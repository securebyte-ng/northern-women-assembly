WHO WE ARE

Northern Women Assembly (NWA)

Northern Women Assembly is an organization dedicated to promoting peace, unity, women's empowerment, education and social development in Northern Nigeria and across the country.

We seek to strengthen women's voices, encourage young people and support communities through advocacy, public enlightenment, education and community engagement.

OUR VISION

A Peaceful and Prosperous Society

To build a peaceful, united, and prosperous society where women are empowered, young people are inspired, and everyone has opportunities to contribute to national development.

OUR MISSION

Opportunity for Every Woman

To empower women and young people through education, skills acquisition, advocacy and community development while promoting peace, unity, religious tolerance and security awareness.
