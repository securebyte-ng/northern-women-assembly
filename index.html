WHO WE ARE

About Northern Women Assembly

A women-focused organization promoting empowerment, education, unity, peace and community development.

Welcome to NWA

Northern Women Assembly brings women together, strengthens their voices and encourages personal growth, practical skills, education and meaningful participation in society.

We believe every woman can contribute positively to her family, community and nation. We encourage women to discover their abilities, overcome challenges and become confident members of society.
